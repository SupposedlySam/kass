Open Settings from the sidebar, or search for any setting from the command palette with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>.

## General

- **Version**: the Kass version you have, with a button to get the new one when an update is out
- **Server status**, with **Restart** if something seems stuck
- **Theme**
- **Launch at login.** Kass starts hidden in the background. This only works for the copy in Applications.
- **Keep history**: how long [captures](/docs/captures/#keep-history) are kept
- **Delete voice recordings**: keep each capture's text but [not its audio](/docs/captures/#delete-voice-recordings)
- **Captures folder**: opens the folder where your data lives

## Dictation

- **Global shortcut** on or off
- The **hold-to-talk** and **hands-free** [chords](/docs/hotkeys/)
- **Microphone**
- A preview of the on-screen pill
- **Sound cues** and their volume
- **Paste at cursor**: whether text is inserted into your app at all. Turn it off to only save dictations to Captures.

## Command Mode

The [Command Mode](/docs/command-mode/) chord, and your saved transforms.

## Transcription & refinement

- The [Whisper model](/docs/models/#speech-whisper) and language
- **Refine transcripts automatically**
- The [cleanup model](/docs/models/#cleanup-qwen3)
- **Remove self-corrections**

## Writing style

Your [styles](/docs/writing-styles/), which apps use them, and style calibration.

## Dictionary

Your [terms and replacements](/docs/dictionary/).

## Features

Re-run onboarding and turn optional features on or off.

## Logs

Recent logs from the app and the local server. Handy when you're [troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/) or reporting a bug.
